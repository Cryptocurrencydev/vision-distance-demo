Near-vision check · Demo

Measure your reading distance with the front camera.

Iriscale finds your eyes, measures your iris in pixels and turns it into a live face-to-phone distance, then runs a short tumbling-E check at that exact distance.

Your privacy

  • Everything runs on this phone. Face detection uses a model bundled with this page.
  • No video or images are uploaded, recorded or stored. The page's security policy blocks it from sending data to any other server.
  • Only calibration numbers (e.g. a focal length) are saved, in this browser only.

Step 1 of 4 · Calibration (optional)

Calibrate once for better accuracy.

Each step is optional and saved on this device. Without calibration, Iriscale uses typical phone and eye measurements.

Lay a bank card upright on the screen, top-left corner in the outline's corner, and resize the outline until it matches the card exactly. This makes on-screen letters physically accurate.

54 × 85.6 mm

Hold the phone at arm's length. Hold a bank card flat against your cheek, beside your eye, long edge horizontal, then tap Capture. Drag the two lines to the card's left and right edges.

This measures your iris size (people vary by about ±4%). The frame stays on this phone and is discarded after.

Measure a distance from the phone's camera to your eyes with a ruler or tape, hold still, then tap Calibrate. This sets the focal length for this phone's camera.
